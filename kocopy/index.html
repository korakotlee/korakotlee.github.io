KoCopy

A small macOS tool that turns things you copy into Google Docs.

What it does

KoCopy watches the clipboard on your own Mac. When you copy a snippet you want to keep, it creates or updates a document in your own Google Drive using the Google Docs API, siphoning long text out of the clipboard and into a document you can keep.

How it runs

  • It is a local command-line program. There is no KoCopy server, and nothing is sent anywhere except directly to Google's APIs.
  • Authentication uses your own Google OAuth client, stored locally in ~/.config/kocopy/.
  • Documents are created in the Google Account you authorize — nobody else can see them.

Privacy

See the privacy policy for what is stored locally and what is sent to Google.
